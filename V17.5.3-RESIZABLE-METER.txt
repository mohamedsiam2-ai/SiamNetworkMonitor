V17.5.3 Resizable taskbar meter
- Drag within 10 px of the left or right meter edge to resize horizontally.
- Horizontal resize cursor appears at either edge.
- Width range: 190 to 600 px.
- Dragging the left edge preserves the right edge; dragging the right edge preserves the left edge.
- Center drag continues to move the meter.
- Lock position disables movement and resizing.
- Saved width persists in meter-settings.json with backward-compatible default 258 px.
- Reset position and width restores 258 px and the default taskbar position.
- Existing fullscreen, Win+D and temporary alert-icon fixes are preserved.
