V18.0.0-beta1 All-in-One foundation
Implemented:
- Data Limit center: allowance GB, billing day 1-28, cycle usage, remaining, percentage, download/upload split, and forecast after two days.
- Connection Inspector: bounded list of up to 1000 active TCP connections and UDP listeners, manual refresh.
- Diagnostics Center: version, OS, architecture, working set, thread count, elevation and local data-file health; copy report.
- New v18 Beta button in dashboard header.
Planned for beta2:
- Native TCP/UDP PID mapping and process identity.
- Application icons with bounded cache.
- Historical chart window and top-application summaries.
- Data-limit notification thresholds, disabled by default.
Stable v17.5.4 meter and ComboBox behavior remain unchanged.
